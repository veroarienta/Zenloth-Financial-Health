# 🦥 Zenloth — Psychological Tools Roadmap

> **From Personality → Behavior → Intervention → Progress**

## 🎯 North Star

Transform the Money Personality Quiz from a static result into an ongoing system that helps users:

**Identify → Understand → Intervene → Practice → Track → Improve**

The goal is not to change someone's personality, but to help them understand and improve financial behaviors that matter to their financial wellbeing.

---

## 🗺️ Roadmap

### 🧩 Phase 01 — Personalized Profile
**Status:** 🟡 Next

**Objective:** Turn the quiz result into something actionable.

- [ ] Show 2–3 personalized focus areas
- [ ] Use primary + secondary personality together
- [ ] Identify the first area to work on
- [ ] Create 2–3 achievable goals
- [ ] Replace “Retake Money Quiz” with “Start my improvement plan”
- [ ] Connect psychological recommendations to financial actions

**Example:** Social Spender + Strategic Planner → set a spending limit before social activities.

---

### 🔎 Phase 02 — Spending Triggers
**Status:** ⚪ Planned

**Objective:** Understand what happens before a financial decision.

**Trigger areas**
- 🫂 Social
- ❤️ Emotional
- 😰 Anxiety / uncertainty
- 👀 Avoidance
- ⚡ Impulse / self-control
- 🧭 Financial clarity

**Data to capture**
- [ ] One or more triggers
- [ ] Emotional state
- [ ] Emotional intensity (1–10)
- [ ] Purchase impulse (1–10)
- [ ] Planned vs. unplanned
- [ ] External influence
- [ ] What the person was looking for

---

### 🪞 Phase 03 — Reflect → Insight
**Status:** ⚪ Planned

**Objective:** Make Insight genuinely personalized.

**Flow**

**Learn → Reflect → Capture → Interpret → Insight**

- [ ] Personalize questions based on the user's profile
- [ ] Capture behavioral context
- [ ] Generate insight from the user's responses
- [ ] Avoid diagnostic language
- [ ] Connect trigger → behavior → outcome

**Example:**

> You identified stress (8/10) as a trigger, were looking for relief, and the purchase was not planned.

---

### 🎯 Phase 04 — Personalized Interventions
**Status:** ⚪ Planned

**Objective:** Use personality + secondary tendency + observed behavior to determine the intervention.

- [ ] Map profiles to strategies
- [ ] Use secondary personality as a strength
- [ ] Recommend specific psychological tools
- [ ] Connect each tool to a financial action

**Examples**
- Social → social spending budget
- Avoidance → 5-minute financial check-in
- Low self-control → pause before purchasing
- Low clarity → define the purpose of the purchase

---

### 🏆 Phase 05 — Weekly Challenges
**Status:** ⚪ Planned

**Objective:** Turn insight into real-world practice.

- [ ] Personalized weekly challenge
- [ ] Track completion
- [ ] Ask what was difficult
- [ ] Ask what strategy worked
- [ ] Adjust future recommendations

**Example:**

> For your next 3 social activities, decide your spending limit before you go.

---

### 📊 Phase 06 — Behavioral Tracking
**Status:** ⚪ Future

**Objective:** Learn from what the user actually does.

Track:

**Decision → Trigger → Emotion → Intensity → Impulse → Strategy → Outcome**

- [ ] Store behavioral entries
- [ ] Compare questionnaire results with observed behavior
- [ ] Detect recurring triggers
- [ ] Adapt recommendations over time

---

### 🧠 Phase 07 — My Patterns
**Status:** ⚪ Future

**Objective:** Surface recurring behavioral patterns over time.

**Example:**

> Your last 12 decisions:
>
> 🫂 Social — 50%  
> 😰 Stress — 25%  
> 🎁 Reward — 17%  
> Other — 8%

Potential future insight:

> Your spending is more likely to be unplanned when stress is above 7/10.

---

### 📈 Phase 08 — Progress & Evolution
**Status:** ⚪ Future

**Objective:** Measure behavioral change rather than personality change.

**Possible metrics**
- Budget adherence
- Unplanned purchases
- Pauses before purchasing
- Challenge completion
- Strategy effectiveness

**Example:**

> Budget adherence: **45% → 70%**  
> Unplanned purchases: **8 → 4**  
> Paused before purchasing: **2/10 → 7/10**

**Challenges → Tracking → Patterns → Progress**

## 📝 Product Principle

> **Do not tell the user who they are. Help them understand what is happening, why it may be happening, what they can try, and whether it is changing.**
